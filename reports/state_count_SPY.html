Decision checklist (yours, not the harness's)

  • Can every state be given a distinct, honest name from its statistics?
  • Does every state map to a different playbook? Two states that trade the same way are one state.
  • Is the count stable across seeds (ARI min ≥ 0.7)? Unstable states are fitting noise.
  • Are visits long enough to act on after gating (avg visit well above your persistence setting)?
  • Does the count survive the walk-forward validation harness (Prompt 9)? Only that settles it.
